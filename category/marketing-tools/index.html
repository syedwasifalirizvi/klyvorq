Maintaining UTM Parameter Hygiene

In Google Analytics 4, query parameter tracking is case-sensitive. If one team member tags traffic with utm_source=Google and another with utm_source=google, analytics reports will fragment the traffic into two separate channels.

Our UTM Campaign Builder automatically converts strings to lowercase, replaces spaces with underscores, and properly encodes special characters to maintain clean campaign attribution.

Measurement Guidelines for Growth Marketers

  • Standardize Source Taxonomy: Decide company-wide whether email campaigns use utm_source=newsletter or the publication name. Stick to one standard.
  • Separate Internal Links from Outbound Campaigns: Never append UTM parameters to internal site navigation links. This resets session sources and distorts bounce rate metrics.
  • Benchmark Revenue Projections: Use the Ad Revenue Calculator to stress-test publishing models before investing in paid media acquisition.
