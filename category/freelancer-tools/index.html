The Reality of Billable Hours vs Working Hours

A common mistake new freelancers make is dividing their target annual salary by 2,080 hours (40 hours × 52 weeks). In reality, full-time independent professionals rarely bill more than 20 to 28 hours per week. The remainder is consumed by prospecting, sales calls, accounting, admin, and professional development.

Additionally, you must account for self-employment taxes, equipment depreciation, healthcare, and unpaid vacation weeks. Our tools ensure you set realistic targets from day one.

Strategic Advice for Sustainable Consulting

  • Price with Revision Buffers: Never offer fixed quotes based strictly on best-case hours. Use the Project Quote Calculator to include a 15% to 25% scope buffer.
  • Maintain a 3-Month Operating Runway: Independent income fluctuates naturally throughout the quarter. Keeping business savings prevents discounting your rates in quiet months.
  • Separate Retainers from Ad-Hoc Requests: Transition recurring clients to monthly retainers once your baseline hourly rate is proven.
