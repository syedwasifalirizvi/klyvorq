Why Accurate Pricing Strategy Matters for Small Businesses

Many businesses confuse markup with profit margin. Markup is the percentage added to the cost price of goods to yield a selling price, whereas profit margin is the percentage of the selling price that is retained as gross profit.

A product that costs $50 and sells for $100 has a 100% markup, but only a 50% profit margin. Using our transparent tools prevents margin erosion and ensures your pricing covers operational overhead and unexpected expenses.

Three Essential Rules for Sustainable Profitability

  • Audit Hidden Direct Costs: Account for merchant processing fees (typically 2.9% + $0.30 per charge), packaging, and return postage before defining your gross markup.
  • Separate Labor from Margin: If you are a service provider, treat your hours as an operational expense so your profit margin represents actual company equity growth.
  • Review Prices Periodically: As supplier wholesale costs rise, revisit your price points using the Profit Margin Calculator to prevent silent margin shrinkage.
