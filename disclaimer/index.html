Platform Disclaimer

Important disclosures concerning mathematical modeling, financial calculations, advertising projections, and privacy tools on KLYVORQ.

1. Not Professional Financial or Tax Advice

The Freelance Rate Calculator, Project Quote Calculator, and Profit Margin & Markup Calculator are designed as educational and exploratory computational aids.

Calculations rely entirely on mathematical equations applied to user-supplied input values. They do not account for jurisdictional tax codes, regional self-employment nuances, specific deductions, or individualized accounting circumstances. KLYVORQ is not a registered accounting firm, financial advisory, or tax consultant. You should consult a licensed Certified Public Accountant (CPA) or financial advisor before making binding business or financial commitments.

2. No Guarantees of Rankings, Traffic, or Advertising Earnings

The Ad Revenue Calculator models revenue potential using standard formulas (RPM, CPM, CTR, CPC). Actual website advertising yields depend on numerous uncontrollable variables, including:

  • Visitor geographic origin and purchasing power
  • Content quality, dwell time, and ad viewability percentages
  • Advertiser auction bidding dynamics and seasonality
  • Ad blocker adoption rates among your audience

Neither KLYVORQ nor any operator claims or guarantees specific traffic volumes, search engine rankings, Google AdSense approval, or revenue amounts.

3. Sensitive Information Scanner Limitations

The Sensitive Information Scanner performs local, regular-expression pattern matching and Luhn algorithm format checks.

It does not provide certified fraud prevention, comprehensive data loss prevention (DLP), or PCI-DSS/HIPAA compliance certification. Validating that a digit sequence matches the Luhn check algorithm does not confirm that an active payment card exists, nor does the failure of a pattern guarantee the complete absence of sensitive information. Users must perform their own final visual reviews before publishing or transmitting confidential documents.

4. Password Strength Evaluation

Our Password Strength Checker & Generator uses the Web Cryptography API (window.crypto.getRandomValues) to generate high-entropy random characters and standard Shannon entropy calculations to evaluate complexity.

While high entropy significantly decreases vulnerability to brute-force dictionary attacks, total account security also depends on factors outside password composition, including two-factor authentication (2FA), device malware, phishing resistance, and credential hygiene.
