FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about using KLYVORQ tools and calculations.

Are all KLYVORQ tools completely free to use?
Yes. All seven core tools on KLYVORQ are 100% free with no trial limits, no hidden subscription tiers, and no requirement to create an account or provide an email address.
Are my calculations and inputs saved on your server?
No. All calculations (rates, quotes, profit margins, UTM links, sensitive scanner inspections, and passwords) run entirely client-side using JavaScript in your local browser session. We do not store, log, or transmit your calculation variables.
How accurate are the financial calculators?
Our calculators use standard, transparent mathematical formulas (documented directly on each tool's page). However, actual tax liabilities, business costs, and advertising revenues depend on individual jurisdictions, market conditions, and operational specifics. Always consult a certified accountant or financial advisor for binding business decisions.
Can I use generated UTM links in commercial marketing campaigns?
Absolutely. The UTM Campaign Builder outputs standardized query parameters compliant with Google Analytics 4 (GA4) and leading attribution platforms. You can safely use them across paid search, email newsletters, and social media campaigns.
Can I suggest a new tool or report an issue?
We welcome suggestions for new tools! You can submit feedback and feature requests through our contact page.
