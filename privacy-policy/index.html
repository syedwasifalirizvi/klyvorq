Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026. This policy describes how KLYVORQ manages your information when you access our tools and services.

1. Core Privacy Architecture: In-Browser Execution

At KLYVORQ, privacy is an engineering principle. Our online utilities—including the Freelance Rate Calculator, Project Quote Calculator, UTM Campaign Builder, Ad Revenue Calculator, Profit Margin Calculator, Sensitive Information Scanner, and Password Strength Generator—run entirely client-side using JavaScript within your local web browser.

We do not transmit, log, store, or process on any backend server the text you paste into the Sensitive Information Scanner, the numbers you calculate, or the passwords generated.

2. Information We Collect Automatically

When you visit KLYVORQ, standard web server logs (provided through hosting infrastructure such as Vercel) may record basic technical connection metadata, including:

  • Your IP address and approximate geographical region
  • Browser type, version, and operating system
  • Referring URL and requested page paths
  • Timestamps of requests

This technical data is used strictly to diagnose network health, prevent denial-of-service abuse, and maintain overall platform reliability.

3. Contact Form Inquiries

If you choose to submit an inquiry through our contact form, we collect your name, email address, subject, and message content. This data is transmitted to our serverless contact handler solely to review and respond to your inquiry. We do not sell, rent, or distribute contact form submissions to third-party data brokers.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

KLYVORQ uses localStorage exclusively to save your interface theme preference (Light or Dark mode). We do not set persistent tracking cookies for tool operation.

5. Third-Party Analytics & Advertising

To understand aggregate website traffic trends, KLYVORQ is configured to support Google Analytics 4 (GA4). When activated, GA4 collects anonymized aggregate usage statistics without tracking personally identifiable information.

To maintain free access, KLYVORQ may display advertisements served by Google AdSense or compliant advertising networks. Third-party advertising vendors use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this website and other sites across the internet. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

6. Data Security

We employ industry-standard transport layer security (HTTPS/TLS) across the entire KLYVORQ domain. By keeping tool computations client-side, the risk of data interception during network transit is minimized.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational or regulatory changes. Any modifications will be posted here with an updated revision date.

8. Contacting Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us via our contact page.
